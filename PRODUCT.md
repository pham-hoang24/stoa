# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: Junction 2026 judges and mentors, who have a few minutes and need to see what the system does and why its answers can be trusted. Secondary: quantity surveyors and estimators who count doors, windows and room areas from drawings and need to verify a number before it goes into a takeoff.

## Product Purpose

Stoa answers quantity-takeoff questions about construction drawings ("how many doors on this floor?", "area of room 2.05?") and shows the evidence behind each answer: a box on the sheet or an IFC element id. It routes by drawing source and format, abstains instead of guessing, and uses rules and IFC data where it can so the answer does not depend on an LLM. The landing page exists to make a judge understand that in one scroll and open the viewer.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, honest. Visual identity follows the Stoa company site (stoabuild.com): near-white ground, deep forest ink, moss accents, grotesk type with a restrained serif accent, fine grain. Structure follows ulrychkristian.cz: a statement-led intro with a key/value block, image-led work cards, one decisive call to action, short notes.

## Anti-references

SaaS gradients, hero-metric stat rows, chat-bubble AI imagery, stock photos of construction workers, cream/sand editorial look, dark neon "AI tool" styling. No borrowed logos, copy or figures from stoabuild.com.

## Design Principles

1. Show the receipt. The drawing with its evidence boxes is the imagery; every claim sits next to the thing that proves it.
2. Abstaining is a feature. Treat "I don't know" as a designed state, not an error.
3. Numbers come from the repo. Only cite figures that exist in the project (gate baselines), and label them.
4. One idea per fold, generous spacing, no decoration that is not a drawing.
5. Plain construction vocabulary (door, room, sheet, lane), no marketing verbs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, full keyboard use, reduced-motion alternative for every animation, evidence conveyed by shape and label as well as color.
